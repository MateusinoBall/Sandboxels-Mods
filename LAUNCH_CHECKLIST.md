## 💡 PRO TIPS FOR GOING VIRAL

1. **Consistency > Everything**
   - Post regularly
   - Engage daily
   - Respond quickly

2. **Quality Over Quantity**
   - Few amazing mods > Many mediocre
   - Great docs > Confusing mess
   - Real community > Fake followers

3. **Tell Your Story**
   - Share WHY you built this
   - Show journey, not just destination
   - Connect emotionally

4. **Celebrate Others**
   - Feature community creators
   - Thank contributors publicly
   - Share others' awesome mods

5. **Keep Evolving**
   - Listen to feedback
   - Add new features
   - Improve documentation
   - Adapt to community needs

## 📞 EMERGENCY TROUBLESHOOTING

### If comments are negative:
- Stay professional
- Address concerns genuinely
- Ask how to improve
- Thank for feedback

### If growth stalls:
- Check analytics
- Try new content type
- Increase engagement
- Ask community what they want

### If you get overwhelmed:
- Delegate tasks to contributors
- Create workflows for PRs
- Use automation where possible
- Take breaks - burnout helps no one
