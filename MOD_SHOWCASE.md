## 🎨 **AESTHETIC ELEMENTS** 
**File:** `mods/aesthetic-elements.js`

Visual effects, particles, and beautiful decorative elements.

### Features:
- ✨ Stardust - Shimmering particles
- 🌈 Rainbow Liquid - Color-changing flow
- 💫 Glow Plasma - Luminescent energy
- 🎆 Aurora - Northern lights effect
- 💎 Gemstone & Crystalline elements
- 🌟 Light Beams & Neon effects

**Best For:** Creating beautiful visuals and artistic worlds

## 🚀 HOW TO USE THESE MODS

### Installation:
1. Copy any mod file from the `mods/` folder
2. Load it into your Sandboxels environment
3. Start creating!

### Combining Mods:
- Mix and match different mods
- Create unique environments
- Experiment with reactions
